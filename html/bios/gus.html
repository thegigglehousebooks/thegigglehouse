Meet Gus

Little paws. Big personality

Gus, looking smart in his bow tie.

Meet the real Gus! My brother's dog lives in the UK and is full of charm, cheekiness and a little bit of mischief.

His favourite game? Snatching a sock or slipper and racing around the kitchen table, hoping someone will chase him! He loves walks, too, especially when there's a treat to look forward to.

Gus loves waiting for my brother to come home. When he spots him, he peeks through the fence with his tail wagging, ready to give him a happy welcome.

And when it's time for a cuddle, Gus likes to perch on my brother's shoulder, just like a little parrot!
