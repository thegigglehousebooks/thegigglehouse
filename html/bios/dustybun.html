Meet DustyBun

Big ears. Bigger Personality

A very handsome bunny!

Meet DustyBun, my much-loved Flemish giant rabbit! With his lovely long ears and cheeky ways, he brings plenty of smiles to our home.

He loves his treats and is happiest with a toy to chew. Although, given the chance, he'll sneak in a nibble of something he shouldn't. Those innocent eyes don't fool us!

When he's excited, he springs into the air with a little twist. It's called a binky, and it's his way of saying, "I'm a happy bunny!"

But this little mischief-maker has a soft side, too. DustyBun loves cuddles, and we love sharing them with him. There's a lot of bunny to love!
