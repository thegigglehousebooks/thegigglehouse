Meet The Author

Kareen

Writing and illustrating as K. Binter

Hello, I'm Kareen, the author and illustrator behind The Giggle House. Originally from the UK, I now live in the USA, where I write and draw as K. Binter.

After chronic illness changed my life, I spent several months rebuilding my strength. I eventually realised I could no longer continue with conventional, in-person work. Losing the ability to do things I once enjoyed was difficult, but writing and illustrating gave me a new sense of purpose. Even on the hard days, when I need to rest, I can create something meaningful. That's how The Giggle House came to life.

A Warm Hello From Kareen

I'm a mum to two wonderful sons, my eldest now grown up. With the encouragement of my wonderfully supportive husband, I'm building a little world of stories, mischief and laughter.

Our family also includes DustyBun, my Flemish Giant rabbit. You might recognise his fluffy face and lovely long ears in some of my books!

I hope The Giggle House brings a smile to your family and a few happy moments to share. Thank you for stopping by, exploring my artwork and joining me on this creative adventure.
